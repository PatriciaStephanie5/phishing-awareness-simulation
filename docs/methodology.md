# Methodology and Metric Definitions

Document the steps actually completed in the Forage simulation and add source screenshots where available.

Before publishing the reported percentages, verify:
- The platform's exact definition of “success rate”.
- Whether a higher value means interaction with the phishing lure or another action.
- Participant count in each department.
- Whether percentages are rounded.
- Whether the overall figure is weighted by participant count.

Do not infer participant counts from percentages or treat department comparisons as statistically conclusive without sample sizes and methodology.
